Website information

Privacy and Cookies

How this static property website handles data.

Information collected

This GitHub Pages website does not contain its own enquiry database and does not collect names, telephone numbers or financial documents. Viewing and offer enquiries are directed to the selling agent through external links.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is disabled until a valid GA4 measurement ID is added. When enabled, it loads only after a visitor chooses “Allow” in the analytics notice. The site records anonymous interaction information such as gallery, floor-plan, map, brochure and viewing-link clicks.

External services

The website links to Rightmove, Entwistle Green, Google Maps, National Rail, Merseytravel, Liverpool City Council and other official sources. Those services apply their own privacy and cookie policies.

Google Map

The embedded Google Map does not load automatically. It loads only when a visitor selects the map button, which may allow Google to receive device and browser information.

Changing your analytics choice

Clear this website’s local storage in your browser to reset the analytics choice. The stored preference is called hardinge_analytics_consent.

Contact

Property viewing and offer enquiries should be sent to Entwistle Green Allerton at allerton@entwistlegreen.co.uk. Website-administration enquiries can be sent to hardingeroadl19home@gmail.com.
